Kolkata trams and the Hooghly: two slow ways to see the city

A tram ride and a short time by the river give Kolkata a slower rhythm. Here is how to plan both, and what to check on the day.

ZikPe editorial 3 min read

Why go slowly

Kolkata rewards a slower pace. Its streets, old buildings, tea stalls and river views show up best when you are not in a hurry. A tram and a riverside pause are two simple ways to do it.

Both are about watching the city go by.

The tram

Kolkata is known for its trams, and services have been reduced over the years. Check whether a route is running on the day you plan to ride, and ask your hotel or check a current local source.

Allow extra time, as trams are slow, and rides can be crowded at peak hours.

Tickets and etiquette

Tram fares have traditionally been low, and conductors sell tickets on board. Keep small notes ready. Make space for others, hold on while moving and keep your bag close.

Ask the conductor if you are unsure where to get off.

A morning on the river

The Hooghly River is central to Kolkata's history. Ghats along the bank are busy with bathers, flower sellers and boats. Mornings and evenings are the most atmospheric times.

Dress modestly, keep your shoes comfortable and ask before photographing people.

Boat rides

Small boats and ferries cross the river. Check the operator, the boat condition and life jackets where available. Agree on the fare and route before you board.

Avoid crowded boats and choose daylight hours.

Bridges and views

The Howrah Bridge is a well-known landmark, and views change through the day. Look for safe vantage points, and avoid stopping in the middle of busy roads.

Photography rules near bridges and stations can be strict, so ask before shooting.

Tea and a snack

Tea stalls and small eateries near the river serve simple snacks. Choose places that look fresh and busy, and drink sealed or filtered water.

A cup of chai is an easy pause.

Staying safe

Keep valuables close in crowds, use official transport and avoid isolated spots late at night. Keep your phone secure on trams and boats.

If a place feels uncomfortable, move on.

Fit it into a day

Pair a tram ride with a morning walk, a lunch and a river visit in the late afternoon. Leave gaps for rest and rain.

Plans in Kolkata are kinder when they are loose.

What to carry

A small bag, a water bottle, a hat, small notes for tickets and tea and a phone with offline maps are enough. Keep your passport and large sums at the hotel and carry a copy.

Light bags are easier on crowded trams.

Weather and timing

Kolkata can be hot, humid or wet depending on the season. Mornings and evenings are the most comfortable. In heavy rain, trams and boats may be disrupted, so keep a backup plan indoors.

Check a current forecast the day before.

Photography and people

People in Kolkata are often friendly to curious visitors, but a camera pointed without asking can cause offence. Ask first, accept a no with a smile and avoid photographing bathers and rituals at the ghats.

A shared smile is worth more than a photo.
