Delhi hostels and budget stays: how to choose a safe, friendly bed

Delhi has hostels, guesthouses and small hotels at low prices.

ZikPe editorial 5 min read · Updated 26 October 2026

What budget can give you

Delhi has hostels, guesthouses and small hotels at low prices. A good one gives you a clean bed, a lock, a shower and a helpful desk. The best ones also give you company and tips. Decide whether you want a dorm or a private room.

Choosing an area

Paharganj is close to the main railway station and very busy. Areas like Hauz Khas, Connaught Place and Karol Bagh offer other styles and distances. Check the travel time and the street at night. Choose a place you can reach easily with luggage.

Dorms and privacy

In a dorm, ask about the number of beds, lockers, curtains, charging points and whether the dorm is mixed or female-only. Ask who cleans and how often. Bring your own padlock.

Safety and security

Ask about the lock on the door, the front desk hours, CCTV and the neighbourhood. Read reviews from solo travellers. Keep your passport and valuables locked up. If the place feels uncomfortable, leave early.

Cleanliness and comfort

Look at recent photos and reviews for bed linen, bathrooms and bed bugs. Ask about air conditioning or fans, hot water and noise. A clean bed matters more than decorations. Check the mattress and the sheets when you arrive.

Ask for the details in writing

Before you pay, ask for the full price, the taxes, the check-in and check-out times, the breakfast arrangement and the cancellation terms in writing. A clear reply is a good sign for the whole stay. If answers are vague or change from message to message, look elsewhere.

Read recent reviews with care

Look at the most recent reviews on several sources. Notice patterns on cleanliness, noise, hot water, staff and whether the photos match the rooms. One complaint is a note, while the same point repeated many times is a signal. Pay attention to what guests with a similar trip to yours say about the hostel or budget stay.

Check the exact location

Open the address on a map and look at the walk to the nearest main road, the street at night and the distance to the places you want to visit. A pin that looks close can be a long, slow drive in traffic. Ask the host how to reach the place from the station or airport, and what a normal fare is.

Food and tea

Ask whether the hostel has a kitchen, a cafe or a tea corner. Check what is open nearby late at night. Tea and a simple breakfast can make mornings easy. Ask for a good place for a first meal.

Meeting people

Many hostels run tours, walks and events. Check whether they are guided by locals and what they cost. Use common sense and go with groups you feel comfortable with. You can always say no.

Planning your first night

If you arrive late, ask about the check-in desk hours and the safest way to reach the hostel. Share your booking details with someone you trust. Keep your phone charged and a copy of the address in a note. A smooth arrival sets the tone for the stay.

goSTOPS Delhi: Daryaganj is the address to investigate

goSTOPS' current Delhi property page gives an Asaf Ali Road address in Daryaganj near Delhi Gate. Use that precise location rather than converting broad "near Connaught Place" marketing into a Connaught Place address. Compare the actual dorm or private-room terms, bathroom setup, taxes, check-in and cancellation rules. A hostel listing or attractive displayed rate is not a quote for your dates.

Moustache Delhi: New Friends Colony, not an assumed GK-I property

Moustache's own Delhi page gives C-794 in New Friends Colony. That is the location to check, not an unverified Greater Kailash I address from an old list. Ask about the actual room, lockers, bathroom, arrival arrangements and guest eligibility. A hostel's claim to be a good base does not settle whether its location fits your first-night transport or daily itinerary.

Joey's: an east-Delhi option near Laxmi Nagar

Joey's own Delhi page describes its location near Laxmi Nagar Metro and shared-bathroom accommodation. Choose it when that base and the actual room terms suit your trip, not because a budget label makes every Delhi neighbourhood equivalent. Confirm late arrival, luggage storage and the precise entrance before travelling. A property's general safety assurance is not an independent guarantee, so read recent reviews alongside its own information.

Zostel and other names: confirm the current property

An official legacy Zostel Delhi page describes a Paharganj base, but a legacy page is not enough to establish current availability or booking terms. Verify the current property and dates through the operator before committing. This guide has not established a current Saket Madpackers or Hauz Khas Bunkin booking from the sources checked. Keep those claims out of your arrival plan until the actual operator confirms them.
