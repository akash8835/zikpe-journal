How a UPI wallet works: a simple walkthrough for visitors

A UPI-linked wallet holds money you have loaded in advance.

ZikPe editorial 6 min read · Updated 26 October 2026

The idea in one line

A UPI-linked wallet holds money you have loaded in advance. When you scan a shop's QR code, the app takes the amount from that balance and sends it to the shop. You do not need cash, and you do not hand a card to anyone. It is the same QR system many people in India use, with a prepaid balance behind it.

Step one: sign up

You download the provider's app from an official store and create an account. Providers usually ask for identity details, often a passport and visa for visitors, and sometimes a photo. This step can take a little while, so do it before you travel if you can. Read the terms on who can sign up, since rules differ between providers.

Step two: load money

You add money by a method the provider accepts, such as a card or a bank transfer. Check the fees, the minimum and maximum amounts and whether the balance expires. Load only what you expect to spend. Many travellers start with a modest amount and top up later.

Step three: scan and pay

At a shop, open the app and scan the printed QR code. The screen shows the shop's name. Check it, type the amount, confirm with your passcode and wait for a confirmation. The shopkeeper usually gets a message or a spoken alert. Keep your screen on until you see the confirmation.

Step four: keep an eye on things

The app lists every payment. Look at it each evening, so you notice any mistake early. If a payment fails but the money leaves your balance, keep a screenshot and contact the provider's support. Most failed payments are reversed, but it can take a little time.

What it cannot do

A wallet is meant for paying merchants. It is not a bank account, it does not earn interest and some shops may not accept it. Cash or a card still matter in smaller places, so carry a backup. Knowing the limits keeps your plan realistic.

Getting unused money back

The National Payments Corporation of India says UPI One World allows unused balance to be transferred back to the original payment source. Other providers have their own rules and timelines. Check how and when you can withdraw before you load much money, and keep your receipts.

Staying safe

Use only official apps, keep your passcode private and never share one-time codes. Ignore messages that ask you to pay a fee to unlock a wallet or claim a refund. Only scan QR codes at a counter you can see. If something feels wrong, stop and contact the provider through its official page.

A sample first day

You wake up, check your balance and head out. A morning chai is paid by scanning the stall's code. A taxi ride is cash, lunch is a card and an afternoon snack is another scan. By evening, you review the list in your app and top up if needed. It feels more natural by the second day.

Questions to ask your provider

How long does verification take? What are the limits? What happens if a payment fails? How do I contact support from abroad? How and when can I get unused money back? Write the answers in a note so you can find them quickly later.

Common mistakes

Visitors sometimes load too much, forget their passcode, scan a code that is not at a counter or skip the confirmation screen. Each is easy to avoid with a small routine. Slow down for a second at every payment.

Transaction refunds and unused balance are different

ZikPe's refund policy discusses failed or returned transactions separately from account deactivation. It says card-statement processing may take four to seven business days and also includes restrictions on the use or withdrawal of refund amounts. Those statements should not be turned into a blanket promise that every merchant refund returns instantly to your card. Keep the transaction reference and ask official support which route applies to the actual case.

Deactivation and deletion are not the same request

The refund policy gives Profile > Settings > Deactivate Account as the deactivation route, states processing within twenty-four to forty-eight hours and says remaining balances go to original source accounts after pending transactions are completed. The separate account-deletion page describes deletion or anonymisation within thirty days after verification, with some KYC and transaction records retained under law. Confirm the treatment and timing of your balance before leaving India. Neither deletion nor deactivation should be used as a guessed shortcut around an unresolved refund.
